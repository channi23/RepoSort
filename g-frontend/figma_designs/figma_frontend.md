# Figma Frontend Design Specifications - RepoSort

## Brand & Identity

- **Product Name**: RepoSort
- **Tagline**: "Organise Fit That Matters"
- **Mission**: From Chaos to Clarity

---

## Color Palette

- **Red/Coral**: #E53E3E (CTAs, risk alerts)
- **Blue**: #3B82F6 (Primary actions, designer role)
- **Purple/Magenta**: #A855F7 (Code visualization, frontend)
- **Green**: #22C55E (Success states, fixes)
- **Yellow**: #FBBF24 (Warnings, secondary highlights)
- **Light Gray**: #F3F4F6 (Backgrounds)
- **Dark Gray**: #1F2937 (Text, borders)

---

## Key Sections

### Homepage Hero

- **Hero Title**: RepoSort (large, bold)
- **Tagline**: "Organise Fit That Matters"
- **CTA Badge**: "Get Started" (red)
- **Icon**: Mascot illustration (panda-like character)

### Features Section

Three feature cards with icons:

1. **Visualize Your Code** (Purple #A855F7)
   - Description: "See your entire codebase as an interactive system graph"

2. **Find Hidden Risks** (Red/Coral #E53E3E)
   - Description: "Automatically detect architectural and security issues"

3. **Plan Fixes Safely** (Green #22C55E)
   - Description: "Turn intent into clear, approval-ready repair plans"

## Component Specs

### Buttons

- **Primary Button**: Red (#E53E3E) with white text
- **Rounded Corners**: 8px
- **Padding**: Moderate with clear affordance

### Cards

- **FeatureCard**: Light background with color accent bar
- **TeamCard**: Solid background color with white text
- **Border Radius**: 8-12px

### Typography

- **Headlines**: Bold, clear hierarchy
- **Body**: Standard readable size
- **Team/Feature labels**: Compact, centered

### Navigation

- **Navbar**: Top bar with RepoSort logo
- **Footer**: Simple footer links (shown in designs)
